OVERVIEW OF INVESTMENTS
The Investment Committee is responsible for managing the organization's funds in a manner that balances financial growth, liquidity, and risk. During the reporting period, the investment portfolio comprised two key components: the Lending Portfolio and the Unit Trust Portfolio. The Lending Portfolio generates income through loans extended in accordance with the organization's lending policies, while the Unit Trust Portfolio, managed through the Old Mutual Unit Trust Fund, provides an opportunity for long-term capital growth and investment diversification. Together, these two portfolios support the organization's objective of enhancing financial sustainability and maximizing returns for its members.
During the reporting period, the club continued to implement its investment strategy with the objective of preserving capital while generating sustainable returns. To date, our primary investment has been made through the Old Mutual Unit Trust Fund, a professionally managed collective investment scheme.
This investment was selected because of its strong reputation, professional fund management, diversification of assets, and potential to provide steady long-term growth while managing investment risk.
The Investment committee continues to monitor the performance of the fund on a regular basis to ensure it aligns with the club’s investment objectives and financial policies. As the investment portfolio grows, we will continue to explore additional investment opportunities that offer competitive returns while safeguarding members' funds.
The investment through the Old Mutual Unit Trust Fund represents an important step towards strengthening the organization's financial sustainability and creating long-term value for its stakeholders.

Strategy and recommendations
In the next reporting period, the Investment Committee will continue to pursue a balanced investment strategy that emphasizes sustainable growth, prudent risk management, and adequate liquidity.
