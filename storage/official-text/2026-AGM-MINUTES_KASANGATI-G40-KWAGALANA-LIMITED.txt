Minute 8: Election of 2026 -2028 Executive Committee members, Chair supervisory and Chair audit committee 
Election of Executive Committee members were; Chairperson, Vice Chairperson, Secretary, Treasury, and Member to the board. 
For Chairperson Executive committee, Ms. Baraza Olivia N proposed Mr. Tabula Robert who was seconded by Ms. Inhensiko K Justine and Ms. Babirye Josephine. Mr. Banumba closed the nominations making Mr. Tabula Robert un opposed hence the newly elected chairperson. 
In the Vice Chairperson position, Ms. Tabula Maureen nominated Mr. Nayoga Ezra, Ms. Inhensiko K Justine nominated Ms. Rita Nakyanzi and Ms. Barasa Olivia closed the nomination for this position. Mr. Nayoga Ezra emerged with 7 votes while Ms. Rita Nakyanzi got 6 votes hence making Mr. Ezra Nayoga the Vice Chairperson of the Executive committee. 
For the position of the treasury, Mr. Tabula Robert proposed Ms. Inhensiko K Justine, she was seconded by Ms. Babirye Josephine. The nomination for the position was closed by Ms. Baraza Olivia and seconded by Mr. Masaba Ralph, making Ms. Inhensiko K Justine a sole candidate and the treasurer of the Executive committee. 
Secretary of the Executive committee, Mr. Jude Tadius Kyobe was nominated by Ms. Tabula Maureen and seconded by Mr. Masaba Ralph. nominations were closed by Mr. Tugume Denis. 
All members suggested that Ms. Rita Nakyanzi becomes the Member to the Executive committee.
