RESOLUTION OF KASANGATI G40 KWAGALANA LIMITED
WHEREAS Kasangati G40 Kwagalana Limited (the "Company") desires to invest in various financial instruments and assets through Old Mutual Investment Group Uganda (OMIG) Limited; and
WHEREAS the Company wishes to open an investment account with OMIG;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT:
1. The Company hereby authorizes and approves the opening of an investment account with Old Mutual Investment Group Uganda (OMIG) Limited.
2. The Company appoints Mr. Mutiga David Brian (head investment) and Tabula Robert (Deputy investment) as the authorized representatives to execute all documents, agreements, and instructions necessary for the opening and operation of the investment account.
3. The Company authorizes OMIG to accept and act on instructions received from the authorized representatives regarding the investment account.
4. The Company agrees to abide by the terms and conditions governing the investment account, as may be amended from time to time.
CERTIFICATION
We, the undersigned, being the (Director and general Secretary) of Kasangati G40 Kwagalana Limited, hereby certify that the above resolution was duly passed and is in accordance with the Company's Articles of Association and applicable laws.
